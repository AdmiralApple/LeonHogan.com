Create as big a Minesweeper board as you want. It includes options for extra lives, plus quality-of-life features like quick-revealing and quick-flagging.

Go fullscreen for the best experience. If you want boards larger than 5000×5000, download it instead of playing in the browser.

The board editor is done. Someday I'd like to come back and add the ability to share boards with other people. The project is open source.
