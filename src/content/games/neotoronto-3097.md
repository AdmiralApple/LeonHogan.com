*You wake up in a city that remembers you wrong.*

NeoToronto — 3097 is an isometric tactics RPG set in a cyberpunk-fantasy city. Choices echo not as numbers but in the way the world answers you.

- **Four Planes:** Material, Digital, Gaia and Divinity. Each one changes how the world responds to you.
- **Eight Voices:** distinct ways to move through the city. Think of them as lenses rather than classes.
- **Persistent companions:** every conversation leaves a mark.
