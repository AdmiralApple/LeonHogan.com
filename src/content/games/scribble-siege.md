An online game for 2 or 3 players. Each player paints an army and some terrain onto their part of the map, within a budget. Then the battle plays out live for everyone.

With two players it's a 1v1 on a split map. When a third player joins the lobby, the match switches to a free-for-all on a three-way map.

There's no game server. The host's browser runs the room and the battle, and friends connect to it directly over WebRTC.

## How to play

1. Open the game, enter a name and click **Create a room**.
2. Click **Copy invite link** and send it to your friends.
3. Start with two players for a 1v1, or wait for a third for a free-for-all.

The host can tune the budget, unit and terrain costs, a draw time limit, and whether opponents' drawings stay hidden until the battle starts.
