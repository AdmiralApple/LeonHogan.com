Take to the skies in an airship and take down those rambunctious pirates!

A turn-based strategy card game made in a week for the East Coast Student Game Jam, by Elwood Hogan and Philip Bixby. This is a demo of the main game, which is in development with better graphics, more cards, more enemies and more of everything.
